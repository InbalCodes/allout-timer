What Allout Timer does

Allout Timer is an interval timer. It runs the workout you build and plays beeps and spoken cues, over other audio playing on your phone. It is a timing tool, not a coach or medical device.

  • Exercise involves risk. Check with a doctor before starting a new or intense training program, and stop if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath.
