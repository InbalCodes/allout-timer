Privacy Policy

Allout Timer · Last updated: November 23, 2026

In short: Allout Timer works entirely on your phone. It has no server of its own, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your workouts stay on your phone. The only data that leaves it is what Google Play or the App Store and RevenueCat need to handle the optional Pro purchase, and an anonymous crash report if the app crashes.

Who we are

Allout Timer is published by UNIQOPS TECHNOLOGIES LTD, Israel ("we"). Contact: uniqops.office@gmail.com.

What the app does with your phone

The app times your workout and plays short beeps and spoken cues. To do that reliably it:

  • runs a workout notification while a workout is in progress, so the timer keeps going with the screen off and you can tap Done, Pause, Skip or Stop from the lock screen (this is why it asks to show notifications);
  • keeps the phone's processor awake during a workout, and can keep the screen on if you choose to in Settings;
  • briefly lowers the volume of other apps while a cue plays, or plays on top without touching it, depending on your setting;
  • uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech to speak the cues.

The app doesn't use the microphone, the camera, your location or your contacts, and it doesn't read or control the music you're listening to.

What stays on your phone

The workout in the editor, your saved workouts (Pro) and your settings are stored only on your phone. We never receive them. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

The Pro purchase

Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details. To know whether Pro was bought, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase service that acts on our behalf. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random identifier the app creates when it is installed (not your name, email or phone number),
  • your purchase history for Allout Timer (product, date, status, store country),
  • basic technical data needed to talk to the service, such as app version, device type, operating system version and IP address.

This information is used only to provide and restore your purchase, and is kept while it's needed for that and for legal and accounting obligations. If you never buy Pro, nothing is sent to RevenueCat beyond that random identifier when the app checks for a purchase. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

Crash reports

To find and fix problems, the app (Android and iPhone) uses Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service that acts on our behalf. When the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, it sends a crash report with:

  • the technical details of the error and the app step it happened in,
  • the same random identifier described above,
  • device model, operating system version, app version, and the time of the crash.

Crash reports never include your workouts or your settings. Google keeps them for up to 90 days. See Firebase's privacy information.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data for advertising.
  • We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking tools. Crash reports are used only to fix problems.
  • We don't require an account.
  • We don't run a server of our own that your phone talks to.

Your rights and deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything Allout Timer stored on your phone. To ask us to delete the purchase and crash records linked to your app identifier, or to exercise your rights under the GDPR or other privacy laws (access, correction, deletion, objection), see Delete your data or email us.

Children

Allout Timer is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date above. If a change affects what the app does with your data, we'll also tell you in the app.
